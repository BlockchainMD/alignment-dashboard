Sources & collection status

Feeds are collected each morning. A failed source keeps its previously collected research. Source summaries are excerpts or aggregator summaries, and have not been independently verified.

How this desk works

Research is filtered by alignment and catastrophic-risk topics, then grouped into a finite daily edition. Keyword relevance helps organize the library; it is not an assessment of research quality. The library keeps up to 90 days of collected research.

No X posts are collected. Reading lists live in your browser. The public site contains only public research.

Updates are requested for 8:17 a.m. Eastern daily. GitHub may delay scheduled runs. “Reload” fetches the latest published edition; it does not start a new collection.
